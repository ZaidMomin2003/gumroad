Welcome to cold mail

Follow these three steps to get your infrastructure live.

1
Point Your Domain to Your VPS
Log in to your DNS provider (Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.) and create an A record that points a domain or subdomain at your VPS's public IP.

Example: app → 203.0.113.45 (so app.yourdomain.com resolves to your server).

Wait ~5 min for DNS to propagate before running Step 2. If Cloudflare, set the record to DNS-only (grey cloud), not proxied.
2
SSH into Your VPS & Run This
Connect to your server as root and paste the command below. The installer will ask for your domain, then handle everything (Docker, database, SSL, reverse proxy) automatically.
Terminal — Paste & Run
curl -fsSL https://coldmail.host/install.sh | sudo bash
Takes about 3–5 minutes. When it finishes, it prints your dashboard URL — open it in a browser and continue to Step 3.
3
Paste Your License Key in the Dashboard
Open the dashboard URL from Step 2. On the Welcome screen, paste this license key to activate your installation:
Loading your key…
After activation, set your Reverse DNS (PTR) record at your VPS provider to the same domain you used in Step 2. This is essential for email deliverability.
