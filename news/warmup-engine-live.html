Warmup Engine is Live — 30-Day Automated Reputation Building

No third-party warmup service needed. Built directly into the platform, free for all license holders.

[ Screenshot: Warmup Dashboard ]

Starting today, every cold mail installation includes a fully automated email warmup engine. Enable it per mailbox, and it handles everything — from day 1 ramp-up through full reputation establishment at day 30.

How it works

The warmup engine sends a controlled number of emails daily, starting at 2/day and graduating to 30/day by the end of the cycle. Each email receives a realistic auto-reply, building genuine engagement signals that inbox providers use to assess sender reputation.

  • Days 1-7: 2-5 emails/day — establishing baseline activity
  • Days 8-14: 8-15 emails/day — building consistent volume
  • Days 15-21: 15-25 emails/day — approaching full capacity
  • Days 22-30: 25-30 emails/day — full reputation established

What makes it different

Unlike third-party warmup services that charge $30-50/month per mailbox, this is built into the platform. Zero extra cost. No external dependencies. No sending your credentials to a third party.

The conversations are generated to look natural — varying reply times, different thread lengths, realistic subject lines. Spam filters see genuine back-and-forth engagement, not automated patterns.

How to enable it

Navigate to Warmup in the sidebar. Select any mailbox. Toggle warmup on. That's it — the system handles scheduling, reply generation, and volume graduation automatically.

You can monitor warmup progress in real-time: emails sent today, replies received, current day in the 30-day cycle, and projected completion date.

Available now

This ships as a free update to all existing license holders. If you're on an older version, hit the "Update" button in your admin dashboard — the warmup engine will be available immediately after the update completes.
