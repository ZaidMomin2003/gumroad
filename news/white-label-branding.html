Full White-Label Branding

Your brand, everywhere. Upload once during setup — it cascades across every surface your clients touch.

[ Screenshot: Custom-branded sidebar + login ]

When you set up Cleanmails, the onboarding wizard asks for your company name and logo. From that point on, every screen reflects your brand — not ours.

Where your branding appears

  • Sidebar — logo and company name at the top
  • Login page — your logo + name, both desktop and mobile
  • Mobile navigation — responsive, same branding
  • Campaign builder — header shows your brand
  • Browser tab — page title uses your company name
  • Favicon — your uploaded logo serves as the tab icon

How it works technically

Branding is stored in the database and served via the /api/v1/setup/branding endpoint. The frontend fetches it on load and applies it dynamically — no rebuild required. Change your logo tomorrow, it updates instantly.

For agencies running client workspaces

This is particularly useful if you operate Cleanmails as a service for your clients. They log into a platform that looks entirely yours. The "Cleanmails" name never surfaces in the UI.

Configure branding during initial setup at /welcome, or update it anytime from the admin settings.
